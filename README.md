# Sales_Performance_Dashboard_Excel

**Project Overview :**
This project analyzes sales performance data for AtliQ Hardware using Microsoft Excel. The objective is to evaluate customer performance, market achievement against sales targets, and product level sales trends to support business decision-making.

The analysis focuses on key business metrics such as Net Sales, Quantity Sold, Year-over-Year Growth, and Target Achievement.

**Business Problem :**
AtliQ Hardware operates across multiple markets and customers. Business stakeholders require a clear understanding of:

Which customers generate the highest revenue
Which markets are underperforming against targets
Which products contribute most to overall sales
Which products have the highest and lowest sales volume

This project provides interactive reports to answer these business questions and support data driven decisions.

**Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) :**
Net Sales (USD)
Quantity Sold
Sales Target Achievement %
Year-over-Year (YoY) Growth %

**Reports Included :**
**1. Customer Net Sales Performance :**
Analyzes customer-wise sales performance from 2019 to 2021 and measures year-over-year growth.
Insights :
Amazon generated the highest net sales in 2021 at approximately $82.1M.
AtliQ Exclusive contributed over $61M in net sales.
Most customers recorded significant YoY growth between 2020 and 2021.

Report Link: https://github.com/pranshunegi030/Sales_Performance_Dashboard_Excel/blob/main/Customer%20Performance%20Report.pdf

**2. Market Performance vs Target :**
Compares actual sales performance against business targets across different countries.
Insights :
Total 2021 sales reached approximately $598.9M.
Overall performance was 8.4% below target.
USA recorded the largest target gap at approximately $10.2M.
Several countries underperformed against planned sales targets.

Report Link: https://github.com/pranshunegi030/Sales_Performance_Dashboard_Excel/blob/main/Market%20Performance%20vs%20Target.pdf

**3. Top 10 Products by Net Sales :**
Ranks products based on net sales contribution and growth performance.
Insights :
AQ Electron 4 3600 Desktop Processor generated approximately $19.4M in sales.
AQ Smash 2 achieved over $11M in net sales.
Several products demonstrated exceptional growth rates exceeding 1000%.

Report Link: https://github.com/pranshunegi030/Sales_Performance_Dashboard_Excel/blob/main/Top%2010%20Products%20(Net%20Sales).pdf

**4. Top 5 & Bottom 5 Products by Quantity Sold :**
Identifies products with the highest and lowest sales volumes.
Insights :
AQ Master wired x1 Ms recorded the highest sales quantity.
AQ Gamer 1 and AQ HOME Allin1 Gen 2 were among the lowest-selling products.
Product quantity analysis helps identify inventory optimization opportunities.

Report Link: https://github.com/pranshunegi030/Sales_Performance_Dashboard_Excel/blob/main/Top%205%20Bottom%205%20Products%20(Quantity).pdf
